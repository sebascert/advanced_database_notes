# Database Workloads: OLTP, ETL, and OLAP

## My understanding

Database workloads can be categorized into OLTP, ETL, and OLAP based on how
data is used. OLTP focuses on processing day-to-day transactions efficiently,
ETL focuses on moving and preparing data from different sources, and OLAP
focuses on analyzing large volumes of historical data to support
decision-making.

## Why it matters

Understanding these workloads helps organizations choose the right tools and
database designs for their needs. OLTP systems ensure smooth business
operations, ETL processes provide clean and integrated data, and OLAP systems
enable meaningful analysis and business intelligence.

## Examples

1. **OLTP (Online Transaction Processing):** An online store records customer
   purchases, updates inventory, and processes payments in real time.

2. **ETL (Extract, Transform, Load):** A company extracts sales, customer, and
   inventory data from multiple systems, transforms it into a common format,
   and loads it into a data warehouse.

3. **OLAP (Online Analytical Processing):** A retail business analyzes several
   years of sales data to identify trends, compare regional performance, and
   forecast future demand.
